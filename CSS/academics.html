OUR ACADEMIC PROGRAMME

Education for Every Learner

At Malizayo Primary School, we believe that every learner deserves an opportunity to learn, grow and succeed. Our teaching and learning programme aims to develop learners academically and prepare them for future opportunities.

OUR LEARNING PHASES

Learning Through Every Stage

Foundation Phase

Building essential literacy, numeracy and life skills that provide a foundation for learning.

Intermediate Phase

Developing knowledge, understanding and problem-solving skills across different subjects.

Senior Phase

Strengthening academic skills and preparing learners for future educational challenges.
