About Malizayo Primary School

Malizayo Primary School is situated in Mid Illovo, KwaZulu-Natal, South Africa. We are committed to providing quality education and creating an environment where every learner has an opportunity to develop their potential.

Who We Are

We are a primary school dedicated to teaching and learning, learner development and preparing young people for the future.

Our Vision

Producing highly educated citizens of high calibre who will be responsible, innovative, creative and adaptable.

Our Mission

Our mission is to enhance quality teaching and learninng for the pupils of Malizayo Primary School.
